# Support – Tempomate

## Frequently asked questions

**1. How does the app measure?**
When you reach the start gate of an average speed section, measuring starts automatically. The card shows your average so far, your expected final average, and how fast you can go for the rest of the section. At the end of the section you get the result.

**2. Do I need an account?**
No. The app works fully without one. With an account (Apple, Google or email link) your settings and trip log are on all your devices.

**3. Why does it ask for background location?**
So measuring continues with the screen locked. A section is several kilometres long and the phone may lock in the meantime. If you only allow "While Using the App", measuring may stop after the phone locks.

**4. How accurate is it?**
GPS is usually accurate to a few metres, so over a section of several kilometres your average is close to the official measurement. This is not a guarantee: road signs and official measurements always prevail.

**5. Why are cameras shown differently in Germany, France and Switzerland?**
Because of local law. In Germany cameras are shown, but we warn you once: the driver may not use this feature while driving. In France only an “Enforcement zone” is shown instead of the exact location. In Switzerland cameras and planned checks are not shown. Average speed measurement, navigation and the trip log work everywhere.

**6. What is uploaded to the server?**
Your reports, your votes and traffic data (with an anonymous identifier, for 90 days), and, if you leave it on, aggregated anonymous road-segment data for the community heatmap. Never the start and end of your trips or the area around your saved places. Details: Privacy Policy.

**7. How do I delete my data or my account?**
Settings › Trip log › "Delete all" (can be restored for 30 days). Delete your account with Settings › Privacy › "Delete account". Sign in again within 30 days to restore it; after that it is permanently deleted.

**8. The sign-in email doesn't arrive.**
Check your Spam / Promotions folder. The sender is no-reply@tempomate.app. The link is valid for one hour and works once; if it has expired, request a new one.

## Contact

Email us: **hello@tempomate.app**. We usually reply within 2 working days. When reporting a bug, include your phone model, the app version (bottom of Settings) and roughly when it happened.
